Features

Everything CurrenciX can do, explained in one place.

Currency Conversion

🌐

Eight Providers

Central banks (ECB via Frankfurter.app, Bank of Canada, Norges Bank, Bank Rossii, Bank of Israel), the European Commission's InforEuro, and Open Exchangerates or AllRatesToday with your own API key.

🛟

Fallback Provider

When your main provider is down, rates come from a second one you pick, and the app says so. The next refresh tries the main one again.

⭐

Starred Currencies

Keep the currencies you use at the top, in your own order. Search finds a currency by code, name or country.

🕘

Recent Pairs

The pairs you used last sit under the converter, one tap to switch back. Swap either way with the round button.

📋

Copy & Paste

Copy any amount, or paste a price you copied in another app: the app finds the amount and its currency.

✂️

Convert from Any App

Select a price anywhere and pick Convert currency from the text-selection menu: a sheet converts it at once, offline, from the saved rates.

Calculator

🧮

Full Arithmetic

+, −, ×, ÷, brackets and percent, worked out before converting; = turns the result into the amount to carry on from.

⌨️

Expanded Keypad

An optional extra row of 00 / 0 / 000 (Settings → Converter). On a tablet or unfolded phone the keypad keeps to one side, in reach of one thumb.

🖥️

Hardware Keyboard

Type digits and operators on a physical keyboard, on tablets and foldables alike.

Fees

💳

Exchange & Bank Fees

Add your card's or bureau's fee and see the true cost next to the mid-market result, globally or per currency pair.

⚖️

Compare Cards

With several cards saved, see what each would charge for the amount on screen, cheapest first, and switch with a tap.

Rates, Charts & Alerts

📅

Past Dates

Convert at any past date's rates, back to 2010 (how far depends on the provider).

📈

Rate Charts

A week, a month, a year, five years or a custom range of up to ten years, with high, low, average, median and the change.

🔍

Interactive Chart

Scrub to see the rate on any day, then convert at that day's rate or set an alert from it. Share the chart as an image.

🔔

Rate Alerts

Get a notification when a pair reaches your rate, checked on your phone after each update.

⏱️

Quick Conversions

Your pair at round amounts, fees included, to copy or share.

Cart

🛒

Prices in Your Currency

A list of prices with quantities, converted into your currency, with tip or tax, a budget and the fees counted in.

👥

Split & Trips

Split the total evenly or by who had what, and group saved carts into trips with a home currency and budget.

🧾

Receipt Scanning

Scan a receipt to add its items, read on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

📤

Share & Export

Share a cart as text, an image, CSV or PDF, or save it to a file to import again later.

Look & Feel

🎨

Material 3

Colors from your wallpaper (on by default), or the app's own paper-and-ink palette.

🌙

Light, Dark & Pure Black

Pick one or follow the system, with an OLED-friendly black.

📱

Tablets & Foldables

Two panes on wide screens: the converter beside the chart or the cart.

🌏

33 Languages

Translated into 32 languages besides English. Hebrew, Arabic and Farsi lay out right to left, while numbers stay left to right.

🧩

Widget, Tile & Shortcuts

A home-screen widget with its own pair and a trend line, a Quick Settings tile, and launcher shortcuts to your recent pairs.

♿

Accessibility

TalkBack labels and spoken summaries, 48 dp touch targets, and layouts that hold at large font sizes.

Privacy & Security

🚫

No Tracking

No ads, no analytics, no account, no crash-reporting service. Nothing about you leaves your phone.

🔐

App Lock

Optionally lock the app behind your fingerprint or screen lock.

🕶️

Private Requests

Optionally make every rate request look the same as everyone else's.

💾

Your Data Stays Local

Settings, carts and alerts live in the app's private storage. Back them up to a file you choose, encrypted with a password if you like.

🔒

Few Permissions

Internet and network state for rates, biometrics for the optional lock, and notifications only once you set a rate alert. No location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone.
