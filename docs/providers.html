Provider Details

🇪🇺

Frankfurter.app

A free, open-source API wrapper around the European Central Bank reference rates. Rates are published every business day around 16:00 CET. Covers major world currencies (~33). Its ECB history goes back to 1999 (the app's date picker to 2010), and the service is highly reliable. No sign-up required.

frankfurter.dev

🌐

Open Exchangerates

A commercial service offering the widest currency coverage of any provider in the app — 160+ currencies updated hourly. The free "App ID" tier provides current and past dates' rates (the app draws no chart from it). A free account is required; the API key is entered in Settings.

openexchangerates.org

⏱️

AllRatesToday

Mid-market exchange rates from interbank data for about 160 currencies, updated about every minute. It needs your own API key, entered in Settings. The free plan allows 300 requests in total, ever; each refresh and each chart range uses one, so pick a long refresh interval or a paid plan.

allratestoday.com

🏛️

InforEuro

The European Commission's official monthly accounting rates for the euro, used for EU budget and grant calculations. Covers ~150 currencies and is updated once per month. Ideal when you need the official EU rate rather than market rates — but since a month-old rate can be far from today's market, the app lists it only when Show unreliable providers is on, labelled Inaccurate data.

European Commission

🍁

Bank of Canada

Daily exchange rates published by the Canadian Central Bank. Covers approximately 23 currencies against CAD. Updated every business day. No API key required.

bankofcanada.ca

🇳🇴

Norges Bank

The Norwegian Central Bank publishes about 40 exchange rates against NOK every business day. Data is returned as XML and parsed natively in the app. No API key required. Its service has regular outages (HTTP 503), so the app lists it only when Show unreliable providers is on, labelled Downtime.

norges-bank.no

🇷🇺

Bank Rossii

The Russian Central Bank lists ~44 exchange rates against RUB, updated every business day. Returns XML data. Particularly useful for RUB-based conversions.

cbr.ru

🇮🇱

Bank of Israel

The Bank of Israel publishes its representative rates for about 14 currencies against ILS every business day. No API key required.

boi.org.il

Switching Providers

Open Settings → Exchange rates → Data provider and select a provider. The next refresh uses it. You can also pick a fallback provider there: when the main one fails, the rates come from the fallback, the converter says so, and the next refresh tries the main one again.

Providers that are often down (Norges Bank) or whose rates are inaccurate (InforEuro) are hidden from the list until you turn on Settings → Exchange rates → Show unreliable providers; then each is labelled with why. A provider you already use stays in the list either way.

If a provider requires an API key (OpenExchangerates and AllRatesToday), the app asks for your key when you select it, and you can change it in Settings. The key is stored in the app's private storage and is sent only to that provider, in a request header, never in the address.
