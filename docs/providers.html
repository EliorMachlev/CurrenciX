Each provider

Led by the currency its rates are quoted in.

  • EUR~33 currencies

    Frankfurter.app

    A free, open-source service around the European Central Bank's reference rates, published every business day around 16:00 CET for about 33 major currencies. Its history goes back to 1999 (the app's date picker to 2010), and it's very reliable. No sign-up.

    frankfurter.dev

  • USD160+ currencies

    Open Exchangerates

    • Free API key

    A commercial service with the widest coverage in the app: 160+ currencies, updated hourly. The free plan gives today's and past dates' rates (the app draws no chart from it). It needs a free account; you enter the key in Settings.

    openexchangerates.org

  • EUR~160 currencies

    AllRatesToday

    • API key
    • Free plan: 300 requests

    Mid-market rates from interbank data for about 160 currencies, updated about every minute. It needs your own API key. The free plan allows 300 requests in total, ever; each refresh and each chart range uses one, so pick a long refresh interval or a paid plan.

    allratestoday.com

  • EUR~150 currencies

    InforEuro

    • Inaccurate data

    The European Commission's official monthly accounting rates for the euro, used for EU budgets and grants: about 150 currencies, once a month. Use it when you need the official EU rate. A month-old rate can be far from today's market, so the app lists it only with Show unreliable providers on.

    European Commission

  • CAD~23 currencies

    Bank of Canada

    Daily rates from Canada's central bank for about 23 currencies against the Canadian dollar, every business day. No key.

    bankofcanada.ca

  • NOK~40 currencies

    Norges Bank

    • Often down

    Norway's central bank publishes about 40 rates against the krone every business day. No key. Its service is often down (HTTP 503), so the app lists it only with Show unreliable providers on.

    norges-bank.no

  • RUB~44 currencies

    Bank Rossii

    The Central Bank of Russia lists about 44 rates against the ruble every business day. Most useful for ruble conversions.

    cbr.ru

  • ILS~14 currencies

    Bank of Israel

    The Bank of Israel's representative rates for about 14 currencies against the shekel, every business day. No key.

    boi.org.il

Switching providers

Open Settings → Exchange rates → Data provider and pick one; the next refresh uses it. You can pick a fallback provider there too: when the main one fails, the rates come from the fallback, the converter says so, and the next refresh tries the main one again.

Providers that are often down (Norges Bank) or whose rates are inaccurate (InforEuro) are hidden until you turn on Settings → Exchange rates → Show unreliable providers; then each says why. A provider you already use stays in the list either way.

For a provider that needs an API key (Open Exchangerates and AllRatesToday), the app asks for it when you pick the provider, and you can change it in Settings. The key stays in the app's private storage and is sent only to that provider, in a request header, never in the address.
