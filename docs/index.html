What it really costs, in your money.

CurrenciX converts prices with your card's fee counted in, keeps working offline from the last rates it saved, and sends nothing about you anywhere. A free, open-source travel companion for Android 13 and later.

Made for the till, not the trading floor

All features

  • The true cost

    Add your card's or bank's fee, for every pair or just one, and see what you'll really pay beside the mid-market rate. Compare your cards on the spot.

  • A calculator built in

    Work out + − × ÷, brackets and percent before converting, or paste a price copied from another app.

  • Charts and alerts

    A week to five years of history, rates back to 2010 for any past date, and a notification when a pair reaches your rate.

  • A cart for the trip

    Prices in your currency with quantities, tip, a budget and a split by person. Scan a receipt to fill it, on the phone.

  • Wherever you are on the phone

    Convert a price you select in any app, from a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile or a launcher shortcut.

  • Nothing about you leaves

    No ads, no analytics, no account. An optional app lock, private requests and encrypted backups.

Open, and checked on every change

  • GPL 3.0 or later

    All of the source is on GitHub: read it, audit it, fork it, or send a fix.

  • Ready for F-Droid

    The fdroid build has no proprietary libraries and is built to be reproducible from source.

  • Security checks

    Android Lint, detekt, Qodana, Semgrep, CodeQL, Gitleaks and fuzz tests on every change; OpenSSF Scorecard and OWASP Dependency-Check watch the supply chain.
