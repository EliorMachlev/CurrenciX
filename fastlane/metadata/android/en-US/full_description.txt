CurrenciX is a simple currency converter: a handy travel companion, not a tool for real-time financial business.

<b>Exchange rates from the source</b>
Choose from eight providers, with a second one to fall back on when the first is down:
- Frankfurter.app: over 30 currencies from the European Central Bank
- Open Exchangerates: over 160 currencies, updated hourly (free API key)
- AllRatesToday: about 160 mid-market rates, updated about every minute (API key)
- InforEuro: the European Commission's monthly accounting rates for over 150 currencies
- Bank of Canada, Norges Bank, Bank Rossii and Bank of Israel: their central banks' daily rates

<b>Convert and calculate</b>
- A calculator keypad with brackets and percent, and your recent pairs one tap away
- Fees: add your bank's or card's exchange fee and see the true cost; compare your cards
- Historical rates: convert at any past date's rates
- Quick conversions: the pair at round amounts, to copy or share
- Convert a price you select in any app, from the text-selection menu

<b>Charts and alerts</b>
- Rate charts from a week to 5 years or a custom range, with high, low, average and median
- Rate alerts: a notification when a pair reaches your rate

<b>Cart</b>
- A list of prices converted into your currency, with quantities, tip or tax, a budget, and a split by person
- Scan a receipt to add its items (read on the device)
- Group carts into trips, and share or export them as text, CSV, PDF or an image

<b>Made for your phone</b>
- Material 3 with colors from your wallpaper; light, dark and pure-black themes
- Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts
- Two panes on tablets and unfolded foldables
- Optional app lock, encrypted backups, and background rate refresh
- 33 languages

No ads, no tracking, no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
