First pre-release of CurrenciX.
- Converter with a calculator keypad, fees, and quick conversions.
- Charts with ranges, MIN/MED/AVG/MAX and a scrub line.
- Cart: quantities, tip and tax, split, budget, trips, receipt scanning on the phone.
- Rate alerts, widget, Quick Settings tile, "Convert currency" from selected text.
- Several rate providers with a fallback.
- Private: no ads, analytics or account; app lock, pinned certificates, encrypted backups.
